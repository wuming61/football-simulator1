# Third-party club badge assets

The 198 club badge files in `assets/badges/` were downloaded from the public
Dongqiudi team pages already used for the local 2026-07-31 data snapshot. The
original page URL and image URL for every club are recorded in
`data/badge-sources.json`. Two reserve teams share their first team's badge, so
the source set contains 196 unique images and 198 local club-ID files.

These images are included in this local v0.9 prototype at the user's request.
Club crests are generally protected marks, and Dongqiudi's image-hosting and
redistribution terms must be confirmed before publishing or commercially
distributing this project.
