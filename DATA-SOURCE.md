# Football Simulator data snapshot

- Source: Dongqiudi public team pages (`https://www.dongqiudi.com/`)
- Collected: 2026-07-31 (Asia/Shanghai)
- Season shown by source pages: 2026/27
- Scope: first and second divisions of England, Spain, Germany, Italy, and France
- Coverage: 10 leagues, 198 clubs, 5,529 players
- Files: `data/dongqiudi-2026-07-31.json` and `data/dongqiudi-data.js`

Imported source fields are limited to club and player data. Club fields include names, city, founding year, stadium, capacity, and market value when present. Player fields include squad number, name, position group, nationality, age, season appearances/goals/assists, and market value when present.

Football Simulator's overall ratings, potential ratings, attributes, wages, budgets, and prestige values are game estimates. They are not Dongqiudi ratings. Three source players had no age and 170 had no market value on the collected pages; the game applies fallback estimates for those missing fields.
